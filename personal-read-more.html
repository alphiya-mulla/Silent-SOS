🛡️ Personal Safety

Learn what to do when you are in an unsafe or uncomfortable situation.

1. Stay Calm

Try to remain calm and think clearly during an unsafe situation. Avoid making sudden decisions that could increase the danger.

2. Move to a Safe Location

Move away from the dangerous situation and go to a secure, well-populated place such as a shop, police station, security office, or another safe public location.

3. Trigger Silent SOS

If you cannot safely make a phone call, use the Silent SOS feature to alert your emergency contacts.

4. Avoid Confrontation

Do not argue with or physically confront an aggressive person. Move away from the situation and seek help from people nearby or emergency services.

5. Inform Someone You Trust

Contact a family member, friend, security person, or emergency service when necessary. Tell them your location and explain what is happening.

6. Keep Your Phone Accessible

Keep your phone charged and easily accessible so that you can contact someone quickly if an emergency occurs.

7. Trust Your Instincts

If a situation feels unsafe, take it seriously. Move to a safe place and ask for help rather than staying in a situation that makes you uncomfortable.
