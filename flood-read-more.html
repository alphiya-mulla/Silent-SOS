🌊 Flood Safety

Learn how to stay safe before, during, and after a flood.

1. Move to Higher Ground

Move to higher ground as soon as flooding begins or when authorities issue an evacuation warning.

2. Avoid Flood Water

Avoid walking or driving through flood water. Water can be deeper or faster-moving than it appears.

3. Follow Official Instructions

Listen to local authorities and follow evacuation instructions and official safety updates.

4. Keep Emergency Supplies Ready

Keep drinking water, food, medicines, a flashlight, a phone, and other essential emergency supplies available.

5. Stay Away From Electricity

Stay away from fallen power lines and electrical equipment that has come into contact with water.

6. Keep Your Phone Charged

Keep your phone charged so that you can communicate with family members and emergency services.

7. Do Not Return Too Early

If you have evacuated, return only when authorities confirm that it is safe to return.
