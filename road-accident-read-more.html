🚗 Road Accident

Follow these safety steps if you are involved in or witness a road accident.

1. Stay Calm

Stay calm and carefully assess the situation before taking further action.

2. Turn On Hazard Lights

If possible, turn on the vehicle's hazard lights to warn other drivers about the accident.

3. Move to a Safe Location

If it is safe to do so, move yourself away from moving traffic and other immediate hazards.

4. Call Emergency Services

Contact emergency services and provide the exact location of the accident and information about any injuries.

5. Do Not Move Seriously Injured People

Avoid moving an injured person unless leaving them where they are would put them in immediate danger.

6. Stay at a Safe Distance

Stay away from damaged vehicles, leaking fuel, fire, broken glass, and other dangerous objects.

7. Help Only if It Is Safe

If you are able to help safely, assist others while waiting for professional emergency responders.
