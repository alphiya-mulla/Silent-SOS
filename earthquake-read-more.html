🌍 Earthquake

Follow these safety steps during and after an earthquake.

1. Drop, Cover and Hold

Drop to the ground, take cover under sturdy furniture if possible, and hold on until the shaking stops.

2. Stay Away From Windows

Stay away from windows, glass, mirrors, and objects that could fall and cause injury.

3. Protect Your Head

Protect your head and neck with your arms or use a sturdy object as protection if available.

4. Do Not Rush Outside

Avoid rushing outside while the ground is shaking. Falling objects and broken glass can create additional danger.

5. Check Your Surroundings

After the shaking stops, carefully check for damaged structures, broken glass, fires, gas leaks, and other hazards.

6. Leave if Necessary

If the building is damaged or unsafe, carefully leave the area and move to a safer open location.

7. Watch for Aftershocks

Be prepared for additional shaking after the main earthquake. Continue following official safety instructions.
