🏥 Medical Emergency

Follow these steps when someone needs urgent medical assistance.

1. Check the Person

Check whether the person is conscious and breathing normally. Stay calm and assess the situation carefully.

2. Call an Ambulance

Contact emergency medical services as quickly as possible. Clearly provide your location and explain what happened.

3. Give First Aid if Trained

If you have appropriate first-aid training, provide assistance while waiting for professional medical help.

4. Do Not Move the Person Unnecessarily

Avoid moving a seriously injured person unless there is an immediate danger that requires moving them to safety.

5. Stay With the Patient

Stay with the person and reassure them until professional medical help arrives.

6. Provide Important Information

Tell medical responders what happened and provide any important information you know about the person's condition.

7. Keep the Area Safe

Make sure the surrounding area is safe and keep unnecessary people away from the patient.
