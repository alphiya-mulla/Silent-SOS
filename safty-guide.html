Safety Guide

Learn how to respond during emergencies and keep yourself and others safe.

🚨 Personal Safety

  • Stay calm.
  • Move to a safe location.
  • Trigger Silent SOS.
  • Avoid confrontation.
Read More

🔥 Fire Emergency

  • Activate the fire alarm.
  • Use emergency exits.
  • Never use elevators.
  • Call emergency services.
Read More

🏥 Medical Emergency

  • Check breathing.
  • Call an ambulance.
  • Give first aid if trained.
  • Stay with the patient.
Read More

🚗 Road Accident

  • Turn on hazard lights.
  • Call emergency services.
  • Don't move injured people.
  • Stay at a safe distance.
Read More

🌊 Flood Safety

  • Move to higher ground.
  • Avoid flood water.
  • Carry emergency supplies.
  • Follow official updates.
Read More

🌍 Earthquake

  • Drop, Cover and Hold.
  • Stay away from windows.
  • Leave only after shaking stops.
  • Watch for aftershocks.
