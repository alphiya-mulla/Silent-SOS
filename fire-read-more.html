🔥 Fire Emergency

Follow these safety steps to protect yourself during a fire emergency.

1. Activate the Fire Alarm

If you notice a fire or smoke, immediately activate the nearest fire alarm to warn everyone in the building.

2. Stay Calm

Stay calm and avoid panic. Quickly follow the emergency evacuation instructions.

3. Use Emergency Exits

Leave the building using the marked emergency exits. Follow the evacuation signs and instructions from emergency staff.

4. Never Use Elevators

Do not use elevators during a fire. Use the stairs or designated emergency exits instead.

5. Stay Low if There Is Smoke

Smoke can make breathing difficult. If there is heavy smoke, stay as low as possible while moving toward a safe exit.

6. Do Not Go Back Inside

Once you have safely exited the building, do not go back inside to collect personal belongings.

7. Call Emergency Services

Once you are in a safe location, contact emergency services and provide the exact location of the fire.
